# Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period unless you're within the 14-day refund window.

**How do I change my payment method?**
Settings → Billing → Payment Methods → Add/Replace card.

**How do I invite team members?**
Settings → Team → Invite by email. Seat limits depend on your tier — see subscription_tiers.md.

**What happens if I exceed my storage limit?**
You'll receive a warning email at 90% usage. At 100%, new file uploads are blocked until you free up space or upgrade — existing data is never deleted.

**Does Flowbase have an API?**
Yes, available on Pro tier and above. Rate limit is 1000 requests/hour on Pro, 5000/hour on Business, custom on Enterprise.

**Can I use Flowbase for free forever?**
There's no permanent free tier — only the 14-day Pro trial. After the trial, a paid plan is required to keep using the product.

**How do I transfer account ownership?**
This must be initiated by the current owner via Settings → Team → Transfer Ownership, or escalated to support with both parties confirming in writing.

**How do I remove a team member's seat?**
Settings → Team → Remove Member. This is self-serve and immediately frees up a seat.
