# Refund Policy

Flowbase issues refunds according to the following rules:

- **14-day money-back guarantee**: Any subscriber who cancels within 14 calendar days of a purchase or upgrade is entitled to a refund for that charge.
- **Annual plans cancelled early (after the 14-day window)**: Customers receive a prorated refund for unused months, minus a 10% early-cancellation fee.
- **Monthly plans**: No refunds for partial months. Cancellation stops future billing; the current billing period is not refunded.
- **Auto-approval threshold**: Support agents (human or automated) may issue refunds up to **$20 USD** without manager sign-off.
- **Manual review required**: Any refund request **above $20 USD**, or **any request involving an annual or Enterprise contract** (regardless of amount), must be escalated to a human support lead. Do not auto-approve these, even if the request is otherwise within the 14-day window.
- **Enterprise contracts**: Refunds for Enterprise tier are always handled by the customer's dedicated account manager, never by general support.
- **Chargebacks/disputes**: If a customer mentions they have already filed, or intend to file, a chargeback with their bank, escalate immediately to Finance. Do not process a refund in this case — a chargeback is a dispute, not a standard refund request.
