# Account & Security Policy

## Password resets
Customers can self-serve a password reset via the "Forgot password" link. Support agents may trigger a password reset email on a customer's behalf after verifying the email on file matches the account — this is a low-risk, auto-approved action.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
2FA is optional on Starter/Pro, mandatory on Business/Enterprise. Support agents **cannot disable 2FA** on a customer's behalf under any circumstances — this must always be escalated to the Security team with identity verification, even if the customer is locked out.

## Account access requests
Only the account owner (the email on file as the billing owner) may request account-level changes (billing changes, seat changes, ownership transfer). If a request comes from an email not on file as the owner, escalate — do not act on it, even if the requester claims to be a team member or manager.

## Account lockout
Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts within 15 minutes. Agents may unlock an account after verifying identity via the email on file. This is auto-approved.

## Data export & deletion (GDPR/CCPA)
Customers may request an export of their data at any time via Settings → Export — this is self-serve and can be pointed to directly. Account deletion requests (right to erasure) must **always** be escalated to the Compliance team — never auto-processed, even if the customer insists it should be immediate, because deletion is irreversible and has legal record-keeping requirements attached.

## Suspicious activity
Reports of unrecognized logins, suspected compromise, or unauthorized access must always be escalated to Security — do not treat these as routine account questions.
