# Subscription Tiers & Pricing

Flowbase offers four subscription tiers, billed monthly or annually (annual pricing = 10 months for the price of 12, i.e. 2 months free).

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | Seats included | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $9/mo | $90/yr | 1 user | Core task boards, 1 GB storage |
| Pro | $29/mo | $290/yr | Up to 10 users | Automations, integrations, 20 GB storage |
| Business | $79/mo | $790/yr | Up to 50 users | Priority support, SSO, 200 GB storage |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Unlimited | Dedicated account manager, custom SLA, unlimited storage |

## Trial
All new signups get a 14-day free trial of the Pro tier. No credit card is required to start a trial.

## Upgrades & downgrades
- Upgrading takes effect immediately; the price difference is prorated for the current billing period.
- Downgrading takes effect at the start of the **next** billing period — the customer continues to be billed at the current tier's rate until then, and no partial refund is issued for the current period.

## Payment methods
Credit card and ACH (Business/Enterprise only) are supported. Net-30 invoicing is available for Enterprise contracts only.
